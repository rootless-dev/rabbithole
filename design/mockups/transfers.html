Rabbithole pause, cancel, download and bucket (approved mockup)

What the new functions add to screens that already exist. Uploads that are sending or waiting get the three-dot button too, with Pause and Cancel upload; two new states, paused and canceled; downloading a finished upload back; and the bucket selector with its list and Create new bucket.

Paused uses an amber ring, canceled a grey one, a download a blue one, so each reads apart from sending (violet), finished (green) and failed (red). Choosing where a download goes uses the system's folder window, which is not drawn here.
