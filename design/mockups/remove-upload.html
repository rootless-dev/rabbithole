Rabbithole remove upload (approved mockup)

One action, Remove, on an upload and on a file inside it. Its confirmation asks whether the files should also be deleted from the bucket: unchecked, only the entry leaves the history; checked, the files are deleted from the bucket too. Files on the Mac are never touched.

Remove shows when the pointer is over a row, and is also in the row's right-click menu. Uploads that are sending or waiting have no Remove: they keep Pause and Cancel upload.
