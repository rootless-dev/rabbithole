Rabbithole upload actions (approved mockup)

Every upload, and every file inside one, has a button with three vertical dots that opens its actions: Try again, Remove from history and Delete from bucket. Remove asks whether the files should also be deleted from the bucket; Delete always deletes them. Files on the Mac are never touched.

The menu lists only what applies: Try again on a failed upload, Delete from bucket when something of the upload is in the bucket. The same menu opens with a right click on the row. Uploads that are sending or waiting have no button yet: Pause and Cancel upload stay in the detail screen.
